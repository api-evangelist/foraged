# Foraged

> Foraged is a community marketplace for wild and specialty foods, connecting independent foragers, farmers, fishers, and artisan food producers directly with home cooks and professional chefs across the United States. Built on a custom Medusa commerce stack, it provides vendor storefronts, product listings, shipping logistics, and secure payments for hard-to-source ingredients such as wild mushrooms, truffles, foraged berries, and sea vegetables. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners. Foraged publishes no public developer API; this file is generated from the API Evangelist catalog profile.
